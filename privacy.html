Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This page describes how the application "youtube-aitoolbox-v2" (the "App") handles data. The App is a private, single-user tool operated by Wenyang Zhou (the "Operator").

Purpose

The App is used solely to manage the Operator's own YouTube channel, Haohaodushu (@haohaodushu), through the YouTube Data API: uploading the Operator's own videos, creating and maintaining playlists, and updating video metadata.

Data accessed

Through OAuth 2.0, and only with the Operator's own consent, the App accesses the Operator's own YouTube channel data. The App does not collect, access, or store personal data of any other person. No API data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, and no data is used for advertising.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and operational data are stored locally on the Operator's personal computer. The Operator may revoke the App's access at any time through Google Account permissions; locally stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked.
